# Agent roster

One contract line per role, committee member and standing role (desk, area lead, judge); every line names
its numbers' source (`agent-scout measure <role>`), its keep/revise/replace line and who decides (the user;
nobody writes its own verdict). `agent-scout install`, `convene` and `review` add lines, the desk adds `use` lines
(`agent-scout log`). Procedure: docs/agent-hr-design.md (5.2 roster, 5.5 verdicts, 12 every agent is replaceable).
